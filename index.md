Engineers and analyzes the trade-offs in large-scale AI systems. This course
connects model architecture design with the practical, distributed systems
required to train and serve them. Covers the full engineering lifecycle,
including data curation, distributed training, inference optimization, and
scaling large language models.

Recommended prerequisite: strong programming proficiency in Python, experience
with a modern ML framework (e.g., PyTorch), and proficiency with git and
command-line environments.
